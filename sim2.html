What Is The Anthropocene Reviewed Actually About?

Because this book is creative non-fiction rather than a traditional novel, there is no single fictional plot. Instead, bestselling author John Green reviews different parts of our modern, human-shaped world—called the Anthropocene—on a 5-star scale. Each essay weaves together fascinating history, science, and John's deeply personal stories about hope, anxiety, love, and what makes life worth cherishing.

1. The Premise

The Anthropocene Epoch

Scientists use "Anthropocene" to describe our current geological age, where human activity is the dominant influence on Earth's climate and environment.

2. The Structure

5-Star Memoir Essays

Adapted from his hit podcast, John critiques everything from Halley's Comet to Diet Dr Pepper—using ordinary objects as mirrors for human emotion.

3. The Takeaway

Falling in Love with the World

Despite human flaws and heartbreak, Green argues that paying close attention to the world leads to gratitude, empathy, and enduring hope.
