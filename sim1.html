Agonist vs. Antagonist Pairs

Skeletal muscles can only pull (contract), never push. During elbow flexion, the biceps brachii acts as the agonist (prime mover) while the triceps brachii serves as the opposing antagonist. Their roles reverse during extension.

2

Reciprocal Inhibition & Lever Physics

When the nervous system stimulates the agonist to contract, neural circuits simultaneously inhibit the antagonist so it relaxes and lengthens smoothly. Because the biceps inserts close to the elbow joint (a Class 3 lever), it must generate much higher tension than the dumbbell's weight.
